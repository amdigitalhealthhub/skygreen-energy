Engineering Quality Assurance

Rigorous Field Testing & Validation Protocol

Every deployment undergoes precise electrical analysis to guarantee asset durability and peak system balancing under high environmental stress.

Protocol 01

String Balance Diagnostics

Continuous insulation resistance verification across all photovoltaic layouts to map voltage tracking arrays and prevent early line degradation.

Protocol 02

Earth Resistance Grids

Dynamic testing of copper grounding systems to ensure impedance levels register consistently below 2 Ohms, isolating lines from atmospheric lightning strikes.

Protocol 03

Infrared Performance Audits

Regular thermal mapping checks on lithium iron phosphate cell terminations and inverters to trace, capture, and correct structural power loss points.
