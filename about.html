Who We Are

Advancing Climate Resilience & Clean Utility Assets

SKYGREEN ENERGY is a Nigerian renewable energy and sustainability company dedicated to providing innovative clean energy solutions while advancing climate resilience, environmental protection, and sustainable agriculture.

Our Foundational Core

We bridge the gap between energy scarcity and farm productivity. Backed by clean corporate accounts, robust internal controls, and a debt-free balance sheet verified by certified Chartered Accountants, our team designs custom mobile solar irrigation assets that permanently move farms away from expensive fossil fuels.

Our mission is to accelerate Africa's transition toward a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable future through technology, innovation, and strategic partnerships.

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's leading renewable energy and sustainability companies by creating a future where clean energy and sustainable development are accessible to everyone.

Our Mission Focus

To deliver innovative renewable energy and climate solutions that improve lives, strengthen communities, and protect the environment.

Risk Management

HSSE & Clean-Tech Waste Security

SkyGreen Energy enforces a strict zero-harm operational safety framework. Standard protective equipment—including safety helmets, steel-toed boots, protective goggles, safety harnesses, and fully stocked first-aid kits—is mandatory for all field operations.

E-Waste Take-Back & Recycle Policy

To comply with clean-tech waste rules, all deprecated batteries or broken solar components are systematically logged, stored in secure, weatherproof on-site enclosures, and evacuated exclusively to certified national e-waste recycling partners.
